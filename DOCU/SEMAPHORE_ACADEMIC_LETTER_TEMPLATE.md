### 1. Project Purpose and Semaphore Usage

This capstone project, titled "LandCert," is a web-based Land Use Certificate Management System developed for the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO) of [City Name]. The system aims to digitalize and streamline the process of land use certificate applications, approvals, and issuance.

**Semaphore SMS Integration Purpose:**
- Send automated notifications to applicants regarding application status updates
- Send payment due reminders to applicants
- Send document requirement notifications
- Send certificate issuance and expiry reminders
- Send OTP (One-Time Password) for secure authentication

---

### 2. Scope and Recipient Opt-in

**Scope:**
- Recipients: Applicants who submit land use certificate requests through the CPDO
- Notification types: Status updates, payment reminders, document requirements, certificate notifications
- Frequency: Event-based (triggered by application status changes, due dates, etc.)

**Recipient Consent:**
- All recipients have opted in by providing their mobile numbers during registration
- Users explicitly agree to receive SMS notifications as part of the application process
- The system includes opt-in consent in the user registration terms and conditions
