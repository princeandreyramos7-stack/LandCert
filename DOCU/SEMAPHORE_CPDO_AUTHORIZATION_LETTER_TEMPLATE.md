**TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:**

This letter serves as official authorization from the **City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)** of **[City Name]** for the use of SMS services through Semaphore for the LandCert - Land Use Certificate Management System.

### Background

The City Planning and Development Office is implementing a digital transformation initiative to modernize and streamline the land use certificate application and issuance process. As part of this initiative, we are collaborating with [University/College Name] students for the development of the LandCert system as their capstone project.

### Authorization Details

**System Name:** LandCert (Land Use Certificate Management System)

**SMS Sender Name:** LandCert

**Purpose:** The CPDO hereby authorizes the use of SMS notifications through the Semaphore platform with the sender name "LandCert" for the following purposes:

1. **Application Status Notifications**
   - Application received confirmations
   - Application approval/rejection notifications
   - Document requirement notifications

2. **Payment Notifications**
   - Payment due reminders
   - Payment receipt confirmations
   - Payment verification updates

3. **Certificate Notifications**
   - Certificate issuance notifications
   - Certificate ready for release alerts
   - Certificate expiry reminders

4. **Security Notifications**
   - OTP (One-Time Password) for authentication
   - Account security alerts

### Scope and Recipients

**Recipient Base:** All recipients are applicants who:
- Have voluntarily submitted land use certificate applications to CPDO
- Provided their mobile numbers as part of the application process
- Consented to receive SMS notifications from CPDO regarding their applications

**Geographic Scope:** [City Name] and surrounding areas

**Expected Volume:** Approximately [estimated number] SMS per month, varying based on application volume

### Duration

This authorization is granted for the duration of the pilot implementation period and may be extended based on successful deployment and continued service requirements.

**Initial Period:** [Start Date] to [End Date]

### Compliance and Data Privacy

The CPDO ensures that:
- All SMS communications comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)
- Recipient mobile numbers are collected with proper consent
- SMS content is limited to transactional and service-related notifications
- No marketing or promotional messages will be sent
- Personal data is handled securely and confidentially

### Official Representation

The CPDO confirms that:
- The LandCert system represents an official government service
- SMS notifications sent using "LandCert" sender name are authorized official communications
- The system is developed under CPDO supervision and requirements
- The CPDO takes responsibility for the content and use of SMS notifications
