### 📚 **Citation Format Notes:**

All references follow **APA 7th Edition** format:
- **Books:** Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work* (Edition). Publisher. DOI or URL
- **Journal Articles:** Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, volume(issue), page range. DOI
- **Government Reports:** Organization. (Year). *Title of report* (Report No.). Organization Name.
- **Online Sources:** Author/Organization. (Year). *Title*. Retrieved from URL

---
