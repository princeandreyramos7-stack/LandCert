## Important Notes

### For the Academic Letter:
- Must be signed by ALL project members
- Adviser's signature is REQUIRED
- Institution seal/stamp is REQUIRED
- Use official institution letterhead
- Ensure all information is accurate and complete

### For the CPDO Authorization Letter:
- MUST be on official CPDO letterhead
- Authorized signatory must have proper authority
- Official seal/stamp is REQUIRED
- Include verifiable contact information
- Clearly state authorization for SMS on behalf of CPDO

### General Reminders:
- Both documents MUST be in PDF format
- Ensure scans are high quality and text is readable
- Keep original signed copies for records
- Follow up if no response within 1 week
- LandCert sender name is already reserved on your account
