  HOW TO GENERATE YOUR ERD DIAGRAM IMAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EASIEST METHOD - NO INSTALLATION NEEDED (2 MINUTES):

Step 1: Open this website in your browser
        https://www.plantuml.com/plantuml/uml/

Step 2: Open the file: erd-diagram.puml
        (Right-click → Open with → Notepad)

Step 3: Select All (Ctrl+A) and Copy (Ctrl+C) all the text

Step 4: Go back to the PlantUML website and paste (Ctrl+V)

Step 5: Wait 5-10 seconds for the diagram to render

Step 6: Click the "PNG" button at the top to download

Step 7: Save as: CPDO-ERD-Diagram.png

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DONE! You now have your ERD diagram image ready for your thesis.

The image will show:
✓ 32 database tables with all columns
✓ Color-coded by category (6 colors)
✓ All relationships with lines
✓ Primary keys, foreign keys marked
✓ Legend explaining the symbols
✓ Notes explaining key points

Image size: Approximately 2000x2000 pixels (high quality)
